# Tiny MLP Policy Distillation Audit

status: `mixed`

## Claim boundary

This is a CPU-only feasibility experiment. The frozen 05C4 seed-2 PHC is not retrained. Train fits the tiny MLPs, val selects one exact objective/width/seed candidate, and NEW3/NEW4 are post-selection diagnostics. NEW5 was not loaded. No HLS, routed timing, power, or on-board claim is made.

## Design

The fixed candidate grid is hidden width `[4, 8]`, objective `['direct_action', 'score_regression']`, and seed `[0, 1, 2]`. Every network has one ReLU hidden layer. `direct_action` predicts buy/sell/hold with boundary-weighted cross entropy; `score_regression` predicts the two frozen PHC scores with boundary-weighted MSE and applies the unchanged PHC threshold. Training uses Adam at `0.001`, batch size `2048`, at most `200` epochs, and val-loss early stopping with patience `20`.

## Decision
