# Hold-Bias Sensitivity of the Sparse Direct-Action Policy

status: `mixed`

## Fixed-formula test

All five terms and six nonzero class coefficients from `sparse_action_base_C0.0003` are frozen. This experiment changes only the scalar bias added to the `no_quote` logit. Bias candidates correspond to predeclared train hold-rate targets of 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 30%, 35%, 40%, and 50%, plus the original zero-bias point. Val selects one bias; NEW3/NEW4 are post-selection diagnostics. NEW5 was not loaded.

## Conclusion

Changing only the hold bias does not keep the sparse formula above the tuned static rule on all three splits. Excessive holding is therefore not the sole cause; the linear decision boundary itself loses task value.
