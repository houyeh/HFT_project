# Tiny MLP Fixed-Point Equivalence Audit

status: `resolved_negative`

## Scope and frozen candidate

The frozen candidate is `tiny_mlp_direct_action_h8_seed1`: 16 current-event causal features, one 8-unit ReLU hidden layer, and three buy/sell/hold logits (163 trainable parameters). The PHC teacher and tiny MLP are not retrained. Train-derived standardization is algebraically folded into the first-layer weights. Val selects the minimum passing precision; NEW3/NEW4 are diagnostics only. NEW5 was not loaded. This is integer software emulation, not HLS/csim/csynth evidence.

## Arithmetic contract

Data and weights use the swept `ap_fixed<W,I>` format with `AP_TRN` and `AP_WRAP`. Both dense accumulators use `ap_fixed<40,16>`. The emulator quantizes inputs, folded weights, and biases, performs integer multiply-accumulate with explicit binary-point alignment, casts the hidden activation after ReLU, and casts the three output logits before argmax.
