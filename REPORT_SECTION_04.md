# 4. Experimental Protocol and Its Enforcement

All numerical evidence in this section is classified as `simulated`: it comes from frozen data audits or software evaluation artifacts, not synthesis, routed implementation, or physical-board measurement.

## 4.1 Split Governance

The evaluation protocol separates model development from diagnostic evaluation and one-shot holdout testing. NEW2 is the development-training split, NEW1 is the development-validation split, NEW3 and NEW4 are frozen out-of-sample diagnostic splits, and NEW5 is a one-shot final holdout that cannot be used for model or threshold selection [source: `n2ew_era/04a1_dataset_registry_patch/results/dataset_registry_expanded.csv`; `n2ew_era/04c_new5_one_shot_final_holdout/results/frozen_contract_check.csv`] [tier: simulated]. The older Phase 01 `test` label is not silently merged into this protocol because its two sessions overlap the calendar scope later assigned to NEW1/validation [source: `n2ew_era/01_full_l2_action_universe_builder/results/build_parts_manifest.csv`; `n2ew_era/05a_phc_lite_microstructure_sequence_feasibility/results/phc_lite_sequence_manifest.csv`] [tier: simulated]. This naming constraint prevents a result from appearing independent merely because two phases used different labels for related data.

The frozen diagnostic scopes are narrower than their registry-level inventories. NEW3 contains six TXFA6 model-ready sessions and 5,656,822 model-ready events [source: `n2ew_era/02e_final_non_ml_feasibility_decision/results/new3_dedup_audit.csv`; `n2ew_era/05a_phc_lite_microstructure_sequence_feasibility/results/phc_lite_sequence_manifest.csv`] [tier: simulated], whereas the broader registry records seven sessions and 8,243,070 raw rows [source: `n2ew_era/04a1_dataset_registry_patch/results/dataset_registry_expanded.csv`] [tier: simulated]. NEW5 likewise uses one frozen session with 815,912 events [source: `n2ew_era/04c_new5_one_shot_final_holdout/NEW5_TXF_ACTION_UNIVERSE_REPORT.md`; `n2ew_era/04c_new5_one_shot_final_holdout/results/frozen_contract_check.csv`] [tier: simulated], rather than the registry's broader two-session inventory [source: `n2ew_era/04a1_dataset_registry_patch/results/dataset_registry_expanded.csv`] [tier: simulated]. Every result therefore carries its model-ready or frozen scope rather than inheriting the larger registry count.

## 4.3 Matched-Random Control

Matched-random evaluation tests whether a policy's result can be explained by selecting the same number and composition of actions without preserving the policy's ranking. The queue-imbalance rule exceeded the matched-random 95th percentile on the reported test, NEW4, and NEW3 splits, with zero of 1,000 random trials reaching the policy result; this is reported as empirical `p<0.001`, not as a population probability of exactly zero [source: `n2ew_era/02d_final_strict_baseline_audit_package/results/exact_random_oos_summary.csv`; `n2ew_era/02e_final_non_ml_feasibility_decision/results/new3_frozen_oos_results.csv`] [tier: simulated]. This test supports superiority to the matched-random control under the same proxy, but it does not establish superiority to another learned model or to live execution.

## 4.4 Why the Evaluation Mechanism Matters

The old-bid-only policy shows that a favorable quote-level markout can be produced mainly by the accounting treatment of spread capture rather than by directional price prediction. On NEW3 at H20, the naive calculation reports total PnL of 1,432,466,070 [source: `n2ew_era/02e_final_non_ml_feasibility_decision/results/old_bid_only_pnl_decomposition.csv`] [tier: simulated], a positive-outcome rate of 90.344977% [source: `n2ew_era/02e_final_non_ml_feasibility_decision/results/old_bid_only_pnl_decomposition.csv`] [tier: simulated], and a half-spread share of positive components of 99.867925% [source: `n2ew_era/02e_final_non_ml_feasibility_decision/results/old_bid_only_pnl_decomposition.csv`] [tier: simulated]. The same decomposition contains 1,515,320,700 of half-spread advantage, only 2,004,000 of mid-price drift, and 84,858,630 of maker fees [source: `n2ew_era/02e_final_non_ml_feasibility_decision/results/old_bid_only_pnl_decomposition.csv`] [tier: simulated]. The result is therefore evidence of a weak proxy environment, not evidence that unconditional bid quoting is economically robust.

The locked sequential holdout reverses the apparent conclusion for the same policy family. Under the NEW5 conservative-L2 sequential simulator with inventory cap 1 [source: `n2ew_era/04c_new5_one_shot_final_holdout/results/frozen_contract_check.csv`] [tier: simulated], old_bid_only produces sequential excess PnL of -553,365 from 30,212 fills [source: `n2ew_era/04c_new5_one_shot_final_holdout/results/new5_causal_fill_sequential_eval.csv`] [tier: simulated]. The queue-imbalance and selected HGB policies remain positive at 722,745 and 893,555, respectively [source: `n2ew_era/04c_new5_one_shot_final_holdout/results/new5_causal_fill_sequential_eval.csv`] [tier: simulated]. These values cannot be interpreted as a direct degradation calculation from the NEW3 naive result because both the split and evaluation mechanism differ. **May be used with split, horizon, and naive-markout qualifier.**

## 4.5 Fee Assumption and Sensitivity

The fee constant is treated as an explicit proxy assumption rather than an exchange-certified fact. **The cost model uses a maker fee of 15.0 (in the same units as the PnL labels) as an inherited proxy constant; the repository does not contain a decomposition into exchange fee, tax, and broker commission, and we do not claim it is exchange-certified. To bound its effect we report a fee-multiplier sensitivity sweep [source: 02b/results/fee_sensitivity.csv] and the break-even multiplier at which the selected policy's excess PnL reaches zero.** [source: `n2ew_era/02b_strict_baseline_and_excess_pnl/results/fee_sensitivity.csv`; `n2ew_era/01_full_l2_action_universe_builder/FULL_L2_ACTION_UNIVERSE_REPORT.md`] [tier: simulated]. For the H20 queue-imbalance policy, adjusted PnL remains positive through the tested 5x multiplier on both validation and NEW4, with break-even multipliers of 16.856332 and 19.372698, respectively [source: `n2ew_era/02b_strict_baseline_and_excess_pnl/results/fee_sensitivity.csv`] [tier: simulated]. This sensitivity result bounds the inherited assumption within the tested proxy, but it does not supply the missing exchange, tax, or broker derivation.

## 4.6 Protocol Implication

The experiment treats the measurement mechanism as part of the system specification. A policy is not promoted solely because it has favorable quote-level markout, a high positive-outcome rate, or a low matched-random p-value; it must also preserve its result under the frozen sequential simulator and the declared split contract. The Phase 02 conclusion captures the resulting boundary: `signal_found_but_strategy_not_proven`, with the accompanying diagnosis that the proxy environment was too weak [source: `n2ew_era/02e_final_non_ml_feasibility_decision/FINAL_NON_ML_FEASIBILITY_DECISION_REPORT.md`] [tier: simulated]. This protocol does not establish real-exchange profitability, but it reduces the risk that hardware optimization is performed around a permissive software proxy.
